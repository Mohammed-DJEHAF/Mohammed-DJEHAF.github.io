My research aims at **reliable, sustainable energy systems**: from high-voltage direct-current transmission and power-electronic control to physics-informed machine learning for batteries and buildings, with an eye on the water–energy–food nexus.

<img src="/images/research-overview.svg" alt="Overview of my research areas around sustainable energy systems" style="width:100%; max-width:760px; display:block; margin:1.2em auto;">

## HVDC transmission, modular multilevel converters and FACTS
Modelling, control and dynamic performance of VSC- and MMC-based HVDC links, multi-terminal DC grids (DC voltage droop and margin control) and UPFC-based power-flow control, including electromagnetic-transient (EMT) models.
Selected work: [MMC-based HVDC transmission systems models in EMT-type program](https://doi.org/10.18280/jesa.550606) (2022); [Implementation of DC voltage controllers on enhancing the stability of multi-terminal DC grids](https://doi.org/10.11591/ijece.v11i3.pp1894-1904) (2021).

## Physics-informed machine learning
Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) and graph neural networks for coupled heat and mass transfer in bio-based walls, with the Institut de recherche de la construction (IRC-ESTP), and neural-network modelling of fracture mechanics with the University of Lille.
Selected work: [A deep residual graph convolutional neural network for hybrid physics data-driven modelling of latent heat transfer](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.applthermaleng.2026.132512) (2026); [Regularized radial basis function for mixed-mode fracture parameters prediction](https://doi.org/10.1177/09544062261478692) (2026).

## Batteries, storage and smart grids
State-of-charge estimation for battery racks and energy management of microgrids, in joint work on the smart-grid platform of IRC-ESTP (91.3 kWh lithium-ion rack).
Selected work: [Optimizing grid-connected microgrid energy management through advanced heuristic algorithms](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICAEE61760.2024.10783352) (2024).

## Robust and intelligent control
Sliding-mode, fuzzy-logic, Lyapunov-based and optimisation-based controllers for wind farms, DFIG wind turbines and nonlinear electromechanical systems, including visual servoing.
Selected work: [Robust Lyapunov-based power controllers with integral action for a wind farm](https://doi.org/10.1080/15325008.2024.2311880) (2024); [Visual servoing sliding mode control with vibration model compensation](https://doi.org/10.3390/vibration9010019) (2026).

## Real-time simulation and power-electronics thermal management
Real-time simulation and hardware-in-the-loop validation (RT-LAB / OPAL-RT), and cooling of IGBT power modules for electric vehicles, with ESTACA'Lab.
Selected work: [A comparative analysis of cooling techniques for IGBT power modules in electric vehicles](https://doi.org/10.1109/CODIT70676.2026.11630975) (CoDIT 2026).

## Sustainability, climate and the water–energy–food nexus
Integration of renewable energy into power systems, climate-resilient water supply and data-driven analysis of climate–health relationships.
Selected work: [Temperature seasonality and spatially structured variation in cutaneous leishmaniasis in Algeria](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actatropica.2026.108366) (*Acta Tropica*, 2026); [Optimized climate-resilient desalination for Algeria's 2030 water strategy](https://sciforum.net/paper/view/27610) (2025).
