Positions
======
* **Since June 2024**: Head of the Automatic Control programme, Faculty of Electrical Engineering, Djillali Liabès University of Sidi Bel Abbès
* **Since November 2022**: Associate Professor (Class A), Department of Automatic Control, Djillali Liabès University of Sidi Bel Abbès
* **December 2017 – October 2022**: Lecturer-researcher, Department of Automatic Control, Djillali Liabès University of Sidi Bel Abbès (Assistant Professor Class B, then Associate Professor Class B)
* **2012 – 2017**: Part-time lecturer, Department of Electrotechnics, Djillali Liabès University of Sidi Bel Abbès

Education
======
* **PhD in Electrical Engineering** (power systems), Djillali Liabès University of Sidi Bel Abbès, 2017. Thesis: *Contribution to the analysis of an HVDC-VSC link based on multilevel converters*. Supervisor: Prof. Sid Ahmed Zidi; co-supervisor: Prof. Damien Ernst.
* **Master's degree in Electrical Engineering** (operation and management of power systems), Djillali Liabès University of Sidi Bel Abbès, 2011.
* **Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering** (electrical energy transmission and distribution), Djillali Liabès University of Sidi Bel Abbès, 2009.

Mobility
======

* 2014 and 2015: mobility internships, Montefiore Institute, University of Liège, Belgium
* May 2023: mobility internship, Technical University of Munich (TUM), Germany
